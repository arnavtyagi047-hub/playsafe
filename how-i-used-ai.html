Development Journey

Project Build Timeline

Step-by-step evolution from initial sports injury research to interactive 3D anatomy, algorithmic triage, and verified medical recovery.

1

Idea and research

Claude • Perplexity • NotebookLM

Researched common sports injuries and the Sports Injury Centre in Delhi with sources. Summarised official NHS and Mayo Clinic clinical pages to establish an evidence-based foundation for self-care guidance.

2

Design ideas and layout

Lovable • Canva

Used Lovable to explore and prototype responsive layout concepts and UI wireframes. Designed the PlaySafe logo, brand assets, and mobile-friendly banner artwork in Canva.

3

Website skeleton and 3D body

Antigravity • Claude

Built the clean multi-page HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript architecture with Antigravity. Claude helped plan the build prompts and assisted with Three.js primitive geometry for the interactive 3D human body.

4

Injury questions and results engine

Antigravity

Engineered the 3-step interactive questionnaire covering 19 sports, acute symptom flags, and the Ottawa Ankle Rules triage logic. Developed the comprehensive 10-section recovery engine with red-flag alerts.

5

AI chatbot

Botpress

Integrated the "Ask the Physio" webchat bubble powered by Botpress, answering athlete questions grounded directly in NHS and Mayo Clinic clinical pages, with 1-click prompt copies and offline fallbacks.

6

Promo video and final checks

Lumen5 • NotebookLM

Produced a short project explainer video from key text using Lumen5. Conducted rigorous final verification checks using NotebookLM clinical summaries to ensure medical safety across all recovery guides.

Safety Guardrails

Using AI responsibly

Sports health requires zero-compromise safety. Here is how rigorous clinical checks, strict prompt rules, and human oversight keep athletes safe.

🩺

Fact-checked

Every first-aid protocol, taping routine, and rehabilitation exercise was directly compared with official NHS and Mayo Clinic clinical pages.

🚨

Safety first

Built-in red-flag alerts trigger immediately when an athlete reports high pain (≥8/10), numbness, visible deformity, or inability to bear weight.

💊

No doses

Zero pharmaceutical dosages, brand names, or prescription claims exist anywhere on PlaySafe. Only general OTC safety advice with pharmacist warnings.

⚠️

Limits

AI models can generate errors. Every page, form, and result banner explicitly states that PlaySafe provides general guidance only, not a doctor's diagnosis.

👤

Human in the loop

No AI-generated text was pushed directly into production. I read, scrutinized, cross-examined, and validated every suggestion before adding it to the code.

  • ✓

Roadmap & Acknowledgments

Limitations and future work

Transparent about where PlaySafe stands today and where it is headed to expand sports safety across India.

⚠️ Current Limitations

  • 6 Core Injury Guides: Currently covers Shoulder Pain, Knee Pain, Ankle Sprain, Hamstring Strain, Wrist Sprain, and Lower Back Pain. Other body zones provide general safe triage.
  • English Only: User interface and recovery documentation are presently published in English only.
  • General Guidance: Educational triage only; cannot conduct physical ligament palpation, manual orthopaedic testing, or X-rays.

🚀 Future Work Roadmap

  • Hindi & Regional Languages: Localizing all triage paths and recovery checklists into Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and Marathi.
  • Nearby Doctor Finder: GPS-enabled directory to connect injured athletes directly to verified local government sports injury clinics and physiotherapists.
  • Expanded Injury Guides: Adding groin strains, Achilles tendinopathy, shin splints, and youth concussion return-to-learn guidelines.
🏛️

Clinical Reference Credits: Medical triage frameworks and rehabilitation benchmarks are informed by and cross-referenced with public sports injury and musculoskeletal health guidance from the National Health Service (NHS UK) and Mayo Clinic.

Bringing accessible, first-level sports injury guidance and rehabilitation roadmaps to athletes across India and beyond.
