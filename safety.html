Safety & Disclaimer

Please read this notice carefully before using any information, checklists, or triage tools on PlaySafe Recovery Hub.

🚨 Emergency Red Flags: When to See a Doctor Immediately

Do not wait and do not rely on self-triage. Go to an emergency medical room or orthopedic clinic immediately if you experience any of the following:

  • Severe or Unbearable Pain: Pain that is unmanageable or worsens despite rest and cold therapy.
  • Inability to Bear Weight: Inability to walk four steps immediately after the injury and right now (key indicator of bone fracture or ligament rupture).
  • Visible Deformity or Bone Dislocation: Visible crookedness, unnatural joint angles, or bone protrusions.
  • Rapid, Severe Swelling: Joint ballooning within 1 to 2 hours of trauma (often indicates haemarthrosis / acute bleed).
  • Numbness, Tingling, or Loss of Sensation: Any pins-and-needles, foot drop, or inability to feel skin touch, indicating nerve involvement.
  • Cold or Pale Extremity: Changes in color (blue/pale) or loss of distal pulse, indicating compromised blood flow.
  • Head Injury / Concussion: Loss of consciousness, vomiting, dizziness, confusion, or blurred vision.

General Guidance Only, Not a Diagnosis

All content on PlaySafe Recovery Hub—including the 3D Injury Finder, symptom question flows, taping guides, exercises, and AI answers—is created strictly for general informational and educational support.

It does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or formal prescription. Nothing on this website can substitute for an in-person clinical assessment by a qualified doctor, sports physician, or registered physiotherapist.

AI Answers Can Be Inaccurate or Wrong

Artificial intelligence language models and automated algorithms are fallible. AI can generate inaccurate, incomplete, or hallucinated information regarding anatomy, healing times, or rehab protocols.

Never make decisions regarding sports participation, surgery, medication, or physical therapy solely on the basis of AI-generated responses. Always verify advice with a healthcare provider.

PlaySafe Principles of Safe Rehabilitation

When rehabilitating any minor sprain or strain, always adhere to conservative safety guidelines:

  • Pain as a Guide: Mild discomfort (level 2-3 out of 10) during gentle rehab is often acceptable, but sharp, catching, or escalating pain is a clear signal to stop.
  • No Forcing: Never force joints through blocked or severely restricted range of motion.
  • Gradual Progression: Tissue remodeling takes time. Moving too fast into high-impact jumping or cutting sports risks chronic re-injury.
