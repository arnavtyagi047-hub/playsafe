How It Works

Four simple steps to identify your sports injury, understand symptoms, and begin safe, evidence-based recovery.

1 🎯

Pick the spot on a 3D body

Tap directly on the interactive anatomical model to pinpoint whether your pain is in the knee, ankle, hamstring, shoulder, or wrist.

2 ⚡

Tell us how it happened

Select the injury mechanism: sudden twist, direct impact, hyperextension, sprinting strain, or chronic overuse during training.

3 📋

Answer a few symptom questions

Verify key triage indicators like localized swelling, weight-bearing ability, clicking or popping sounds, and range of motion.

4 🏁

Get your recovery plan

Receive instant first-aid recommendations (PRICE protocol), structured rehab phases, and clear milestones for returning to sport.
